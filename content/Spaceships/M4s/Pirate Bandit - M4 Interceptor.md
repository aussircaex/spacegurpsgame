No two pirate ships are the same, but they tend to be retrofitted to certain standards anyway to fill their intended roles. The statistics presented here are an average short-range interceptor, often docked aboard a [[Caravel-class M7 Patrol Carrier]]. Pirates pick their targets carefully, allowing Bandits to forgo shielding, and they lack FTL drives, returning to local ports or carriers after their flights. They notably sacrifice valuable space to make room for a small cargo area, as pirates never want to be unable to seize booty from their prey.

Most Bandits have no life support systems, requiring their pilots to wear vacuum suits while operating them.
