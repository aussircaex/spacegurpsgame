Ships are rated for their mass category - in the game rules, they are referred to as "Size Modifier", so a 30-ton ship is "SM+5". In-universe, they are given the designation "MX", where X is the mass category. So a 300-ton vessel (SM+7) is an "M7" craft.

Spaceships come in a variety of configurations and styles, but here are some common terms and categories you can expect to see in the Terran Sector:

##### Fighters
**M4** (10 tons, 30-50'): Scout, Light Interceptor
**M5** (30 tons, 45-75'): Heavy Interceptor, Superiority Fighter, Fighter-Bomber
**M6** (100 tons, 60-100'): Gunship, Heavy Bomber
##### Warships
**M7** (300 tons, 90-150'): PT Boat, FAC, Patrol Carrier
**M8** (1000 tons, 150-250'): Cutter
**M9** (3000 tons, 200-330'): Corvette, Assault Carrier
**M10** (10,000 tons, 300-500'): Frigate
**M11** (30,000 tons, 450-750'): Destroyer
**M12** (100,000 tons, 600-1000'): Cruiser, Escort Carrier
##### Capital Ships
**M13** (300,000 tons, 900-1500'): Battlecruiser
**M14** (1,000,000 tons, 1500-2500'): Battleship
**M15** (3,000,000 tons, 2000-3300'): Dreadnought, Carrier, Fleet Carrier
##### Freighters
**M5, M6, M7**: Light Freighter, Courier, Shuttle, Bus
**M8, M9**: Freighter, Medium Freighter, "Macks"
**M10, M11, M12**: Heavy Freighter, Long-Hauler
**M13**: Container Ship, Tanker
