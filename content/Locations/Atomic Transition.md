Located in some far-flung place in the universe, this space station is accessible through the 2nd generation Jump Gate network.. if you have the IFF codes to pass through.

The operators all wear coverings - the guards with face-concealing helmets, and the vendors with masks and cloaks. It's possible they're not human - they are too tall and their limbs too long, but they look more human-like than any alien species in the region.

Tier 1 clients like you can access their weapons store, but the station clearly also has a shipyard for custom modifications to spacecraft. Perhaps you can access their services if you gain more of their trust…

_Note: if a weapon isn't listed here that you are interested in, feel free to ask the DM. Prices are generally 2x the prince in a sourcebook for LC2 items._
### Ammunition

- Standard ammunition (Full metal jacket rounds, shells, rifled slugs) - normal price
- Hollow-point ammunition - 1x normal price
- Armor-piercing ammunition - 2x normal price. LC2
- ADPS slugs (shotguns only) - treat as a slug, except the damage becomes 6d(2) pi+ - 3x normal price
