Located in some far-flung place in the universe, this space station is accessible through the 2nd generation Jump Gate network.. if you have the IFF codes to pass through.

The operators all wear coverings - the guards with face-concealing helmets, and the vendors with masks and cloaks. It's possible they're not human - they are too tall and their limbs too long, but they look more human-like than any alien species in the region.

Tier 1 clients like you can access their weapons store, but the station clearly also has a shipyard for custom modifications to spacecraft. Perhaps you can access their services if you gain more of their trust…

_Note: if a weapon isn't listed here that you are interested in, feel free to ask the DM. Prices are generally 2x the prince in a sourcebook for LC2 items._
### Ammunition

- Standard ammunition (Full metal jacket rounds, shells, rifled slugs) - normal price
- Hollow-point ammunition - 1x normal price
- Armor-piercing ammunition - 2x normal price. LC2
- ADPS slugs (shotguns only) - treat as a slug, except the damage becomes 6d(2) pi+ - 3x normal price

## Armor

### Shields
* **Force Shield**: Defense Bonus: 3, 0.5lbs, $1500. This is a whole-body barrier, not a handheld shield, so it provides its Defense Bonus automatically from all sides, not just the front and side.
	* *Note: most of you probably already have one of these. To make sure you don't forget you have it, you can go into the item on your GCS sheet - change its Features from "Gives a conditional modifier" to "Gives an attribute modifier" to Dodge, Block, and Parry.
	* *Also note: most enemies won't have these. The intention is to give you guys a "free" +3 to your defenses to intentionally make combat less deadly for you*

### Concealable Armor
* **Tailored Clothing Armor**: "Reflex", a futuristic improvement on Kevlar, can be woven into any textile. You can have *Atomic Transition's* tailors make you a suit of armored clothing that passes for ordinary clothes in every way.
	* This is done the same way as the "Kev-Max Armored Clothing" option in [[1 - Armor, Common]]
* The "base values" are $1200, 8lbs, and 12/4 DR, but that's always modified below, so keep reading!
* Decide the outfit you want, then check the Coverage image to add up the % of your body the outfit covers. Common values are **0.25** for a t-shirt or vest, **0.35** for pants, **0.375** for a long-sleeved shirt or a jacket, 0.725 for a trench coat outfit (coat+pants+shirt), 0.9 for a full-body outfit (including gloves and shoes) but no head covering.![[coverage.png]]
* Now decide if you want a **Normal**, **Light**, or **Diaphanous** style for your outfit
	* Normal: x1 DR, weight, and cost. Must be worn as outerwear.
	* Light: x2/3 DR, weight, and cost. Can be worn *under* clothing.
	* Diaphanous: 1/2 DR, weight, and cost. This is for things like swimsuits or underwear if you desire.
* Finally, you can pay 4x for a "stylish" outfit, appropriate for occasions where being well-dressed is expected (e.g. a tailored suit for a dinner party), or 20x for a "fashion original" if you want to truly stand out (good for +2 on Influence checks where relevant)
* **Example 1**: Johnny wants his regular pilot's outfit, with bomber jacket, gloves, jeans, and boots to be armored. That's 90% coverage. It's his outerwear, so it's Normal, and it doesn't need to be stylish. It costs $1,080, has DR 12/4, weighs 7.2lbs, and can be worn like normal clothes.
* **Example 2:** Carina wants to dress as Pamela Anderson in *Baywatch* for Halloween, but also doesn't want to get shot. That's a 35% coverage outfit (torso and groin), and she chooses Diaphanous so she can wear pants and a shirt over it while walking to the party. She wants to compete for Best Costume, so she gets a Stylish outfit. It costs (1200 x 0.35 x 0.5 x 4) = $840, has DR 6/2, weighs 1.4lbs, and can be worn under other clothing.
### Heavy Tailored Armor
* You can use the same rules as above for **Concealable Armor** to make a custom outfit **Heavy** instead of Normal/Light/Diaphanous.
* It is separated out here because it *cannot be mistaken for normal clothing*. It is obviously a protective outfit, but it still can follow your fashion desires.
* Multiply DR and weight by 1.5, and cost by 3 (the premium due to it being LC2)
* **Example:** Cdub wants to keep his trademark blue-collar style, but wants the best protection he can get with it. His sleeveless trench coat, shirt, jeans, and boots are 70% coverage, and he gets it made Heavy and a normal cut. It costs (1200 x 0.7 x 3) = $2,520, provides DR 18/6, and weighs 8.4lbs.
