A mysterious robot discovered aboard Listening Post X7Q and named "PAL" by the crew of the *Auntie Ann*. PAL is childlike in disposition and rather naive, but is eager to do right by those they consider friends (which seems to be everyone, so far).

When the crew met him, he was intelligent enough to interface with the station's systems, but did not realize that he was trapped in a box.

They are roughly football-shaped, with no limbs, but capable of anti-grav flight and equipped with a zero-point energy beam and computer interface hardware (i.e. spacefuture ethernet/USB/HDMI cabling).

They have demonstrated the ability to consume raw materials - scrap metals, at the very least - and perform machinist work within their body. They have successfully replicated a simple wrench, but not a more advanced engine part.

- A portion of a metal table was found gone near where PAL was loitering, and he seemed to increase in size slightly; more parts appeared on his chassis.
- Agent Krane of the TBI requested that the *Ann* take PAL with them, to mitigate the risk of losing two valuable assets - being PAL and LPX7Q itself - at the same time in the event of an attack. In exchange, she pulled strings to erase the *Ann*'s blacklist status on the Terran BBS.
- The crew pre-emptively instructed PAL not to plug into the ship, interface with its systems, or consume anything unless they gave him permission to do so. He seemed amenable.
- The crew also pre-emptively instructed PAL not to speak to anyone other than the crew. He obeys inasmuch as he does not initiate conversation with anyone, but tends to forget this instruction when spoken to; replying with his trademark "Hello!"  He also tends to investigate when new people arrive on the ship, despite crew instructions.
- He tends to rest in the Force Shield module. While the crew never noticed themselves, PAL informed [[Jdzzriiffdskrmxtl (Joe)|Joe]] that he disabled the surveillance equipment in his preferred corner.
- PAL regularly displays tiredness and exhaustion, especially after being asked to create objects. This is not a trait most robots possess.
