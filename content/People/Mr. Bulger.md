
An organized crime boss based on Tuatha Prime. Mr. Bulger has an infant granddaughter and has strong-armed the girl's father into moving to Tuatha Prime and working for him, rather than be the horrible deadbeat he was.

- The crew of the Auntie Ann, not wanting to be stiffed by a bad check, confronted Mr. Bulger while he dined at a favorite restaurant, and managed to impress him with their moxie.
