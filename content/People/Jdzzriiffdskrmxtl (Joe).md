A Lycosian from near the star Polaris, Joe is a social scientist of sorts, seeking to write about human history, sociology, and anthropology. The crew of the *Auntie Ann* contacted him to take a job ferrying him to Earth on his research mission, and then to return him to the Descant system, where his ship has been impounded by local authorities, and is not permitted to retrieve it unless he immediately flies it out of the Terran Sector.

His people, named Lycosians in English due to their spider-like qualities, are mostly humanoid in shape, but with four legs, a mane of sorts, wide-set eyes, and spider-like fangs and mouthparts. Their languages are theoretically comprehensible to human ears, but they are nearly impossible to reproduce with human vocal abilities, and vice-versa. Fortunately, translation software is available, with near-instantaneous translation allowing for real-time conversation, but the quality ranges between "impenetrable accent" and "understandable but with confusing syntax". The Lycosians were once also subjects of the [[The Universal Union|Universal Union]], and given their proximity to the Terran Sector, Lycosians became familiar to humanity as members of the UU's armies.

While it is certainly possible that there is a translation error involved, Joe routinely refers to his research as concerning humanity "and also dogs". Earth (sometimes mistranslated as "Dirt") is the homeworld of "humans and also dogs". Given he has a positive but not extraordinary reaction to dogs he's seen so far, you're not sure what the significance of this is.

- The crew successfully negotiated with Joe; he agreed to a fee of $5000 per day, plus expenses, to pay for his research trip to Earth, with the option to extend the trip to other locations. He prefers to settle his debt at the conclusion of the trip to simplify bookkeeping, but has brought his valuable iridium for barter in case there is a cash-flow problem.
- Joe may have discovered the presence of two AGIs aboard the *Auntie Ann*. Carina successfully bluffed that they are both merely highly-sophisticated VIs, but new evidence may raise his suspicions again.
	- He has interviewed PAL on at least one occasion; no one in the crew succeeded at an Observation check to notice when he did so.
- When visiting Creyt, an icy planet with only a few major settlements, Joe asked to visit a local bar to test his hypothesis that "cold temperatures reduce human hospitality". While never doing anything wrong, per se, he did not endear himself to the locals, and a "we don't take kindly to your types around here" situation was defused, fortunately, by Carina.
- The *Auntie Ann* happened to arrive in New Ireland on Easter, and Joe was positively exuberant to take part in "human cultural event". After an argument where the crew of the *Ann* tried to ask for additional security fees, Joe decided to hire the services of the Wild Geese as escorts instead. He had a delightful time on New Ireland, enjoying the parades, Guinness beer and whiskey, and returned to the *Ann* with a cart full of merchandise.
