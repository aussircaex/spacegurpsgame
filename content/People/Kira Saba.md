Scientific progress, as Dr. Saba would readily agree, is done through the combined contributions of humanity, and no one person is responsible for the discoveries that shape history.

With that said, Kira Saba was the physicist whose research finally produced the "Jump Equations"; the scientific theory that allows for instantaneous travel between two points in space-time, which led directly to the engineering and construction of the [[Jump Gate|Jump Gates]].

She was well into her 110s when the [[The Universal Union|Universal Union]] attacked, yet she was still able to lead humanity's efforts to shut down the Gates and to destroy all of her research so as to keep it out of the Union's hands.

While the story of this heroic action has been dramatized in media dozens of times (including an award-winning performance for actress Skreen Argenta), Dr. Saba herself disappeared in the chaos of the initial invasion. Given her age, she was presumed to either have died, or to have gone into hiding for the rest of her years.
