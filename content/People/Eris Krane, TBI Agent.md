The crew of the *Auntie Ann* met Agent Krane undercover as a fellow freelance employee of Nirvana Terraforming and Stellar Engineering. She appears to be in her early 30s and claimed to have spent most of her career drifting around the Sol system doing a mix of difficult, dangerous, and humiliating jobs.

In truth, Commander or Agent Krane is part of the Terran Bureau of Investigation, a clandestine group whose existence and purpose is still a mystery to the *Ann's* crew. She had enough clout to clear the *Ann's* blacklisting from the Terran BBS, but while helpful, is not necessarily indicative of overwhelming amounts of power or influence.

As beautiful as she is dangerous, Krane is known to be highly trained in subterfuge, espionage, and all manner of skills necessary for an intelligence officer of her caliber.

- The crew endeared themselves well to Krane during her stay on the *Ann*.
- Krane requested that the crew take on [[PAL]]. The Listening Post X7Q was a significant intelligence discovery, as was PAL themselves, and so Krane desired to keep them separated so they would not both be lost in the event of an attack. The crew accepted.
