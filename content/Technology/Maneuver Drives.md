
- **[[Travel Drive]]**: A ship's standard Travel Drive also serves as a basic sub-light engine. However, accelerating to speeds beyond 50mps triggers the engine's negative-mass functions (which is what allow for FTL travel), and so cruising speed for most ships arriving at their destinations is capped at that speed. They are capable of about 5G of acceleration, and use negligible amounts of fuel to do so. However, any stress on the engine (from impacts or from weapons fire) immediately disengages them - cruising velocity is maintained, but the ship must rely on sub-light reaction engines for maneuvering in these situations. Travel drives are only capable of forward thrust/deceleration, so all ships must also be equipped with standard reaction engines to achieve their course and for landing and taking off from planets.

- **Fusion Engine, Water-Fueled**: Common on freighters and civilian craft, but also on warships, these engines have low acceleration and fuel efficiency, but operate with water-based fuel, which is extremely inexpensive. These ships generally only rely on their maneuver engines for take-off and landing and can otherwise rely on their Travel Drives for cruising. This engine type is common on warships because hydrogen or antimatter fuel costs on craft larger than corvettes quickly grow prohibitive. They offer 1.5G of acceleration and 45mps delta-v per fuel tank.

- **Super Fusion Torch, Hydrogen-Fueled:** Requiring more costly hydrogen fuel, this engine is fast and tremendously efficient, offering either 50G or 100G acceleration with 450 or 225 delta-v per fuel tank, respectively. Since such high velocities are generally not necessary for standard interstellar travel due to Travel Drives, they are mostly found on fighter craft and corvettes (SM +4-+7). Larger warships were constructed with these drives to fight the Universal Union, but as hydrogen fuel is one hundred times as expensive as water-based fuel, those large engines have largely been decommissioned.

- **Super Antimatter Plasma Torch**: Usually seen only on high-performance interceptors or scout ships, this engine is actually somewhat less expensive to construct, but relies on highly expensive antimatter fuel. They are capable of 100G or 200G acceleration with 360 or 180 delta-v per fuel tank, respectively.
