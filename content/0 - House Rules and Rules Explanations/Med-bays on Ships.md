By default, a Med bay, or Sick bay, takes the place of one Cabin in a Habitat module on a spaceship. Habitat modules have multiple Cabins available depending on the size of the ship; see Spaceships page 17.

By default, a Sickbay comes equipped with:
* Medical Bed (UT199)  
* Diagnostic Bed (UT197)  
* Emergency Support Unit (Hospital ESU) (UT198)  
* Surgical Equipment (Operating Theater) (UT200)  
* Medical supplies sufficient for 50 patient-days of Physician treatment (UT199).  
  
You can purchase additional equipment for your medbay of TL10 or lower that is not marked with a ^ for super-science. We are at TL10 for the purposes of equipment that improves with TL.  
  
For each Cabin used for your Sick Bay, you have space to accommodate one patient. If you want to add equipment such as a Regeneration Tank, that counts as occupying one of these spaces (so if you have two Cabins worth of space and a Regeneration tank, you can't treat more than one patient at once unless the second is using the tank.)
